# Data model

Locked in 13 · Data model, 2026-11-12. Words: [GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Stack: 07 (D1 via Drizzle), money as whole minor units ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-money-as-whole-minor-units.md)).

## Rules for every table

- `id`: text UUIDv7, **made on the phone** (lets the phone show a new row at once, and keeps offline sync open later).
- `user_id`: owner, references Better Auth's `user.id`; on every row; every query scoped by the one shared check (08). Delete-account deletes by `user_id` (cascade).
- `created_at`, `updated_at`: UTC instants.
- Money: `<x>_minor` integer + `currency` (ISO 4217 code). Rates: decimal text exactly as typed.
- Days: `*_on` columns are plain `YYYY-MM-DD` in the home time zone.
- Edits and deletes are real updates and deletes (no change history in v1).

## Login tables (owned by Better Auth)

`user`, `session`, `account`, `verification`, `passkey`. Note: Better Auth's `account` means "a linked sign-in method", **not** the glossary's Account; in code the glossary Account is the `user` row.

## Only ever worked out (never stored)

Free money, daily allowance, days left, spending speed, run-out verdict, pot amount left and verdict, savings totals (per currency and combined), card balances, plan progress and interest, prepayment saving, leftover before correction, overspend, rate gain and loss, commitment "still to pay".

## How things link

- **Pay cycle id**: its own `cycle` row, made when the salary is entered. A cycle ends when the next one starts; the newest is the current one.
- **Recurring commitments**: on salary day each active `recurring_commitment` makes a `commitment` row in the new cycle, filled with the last amount and date, for the user to check. The template is kept in step with the commitment its Every cycle switch is on for (`commitment.recurring_id`); deleting that commitment deletes the template. `usual_day` is a day of the month (1–31). Kinds: bill, savings, card_bill (item 18: `card_id` set; filled from the card balance, not `usual_amount_minor`).
- **Pots**: the `pot` lives across cycles; each cycle gets its own `pot_allocation` (at most one per pot and cycle). A spend's `pot_id` has no foreign key: a spend whose pot is gone counts as free money.
- **Two currencies** (item 15): first use stores one `opening` income per currency and the first `counting_rate` (also the first cycle's `reserve_rate`). Salary day stores the counting rate as the new cycle's `reserve_rate`; a rate typed in step 6 (ticket 38) is first saved as a new `counting_rate` row in the same write, stamped like the rest, so it is that rate. The ended cycle's salary-currency leftover (item 16) is a second `leftover` income in that currency (`to_savings_minor` = dollars saved; amount = dollars kept + carried dollar commitments' money), with a second `cycle_close` row in that currency (correction, saved). The undo record is on the spending currency's row only.
- **Conversion** (item 16): one `conversion` row plus a `counting_rate` row with the same id and its rate, in one batch; Undo deletes both. `covered_minor` and `gain_minor` are worked out by the money rules when saved and kept (later conversions see it). In free money it counts at once (not by `done_on`): dollars out, rupees in, the gain out of free money. A gain is in the rupee savings total (`gain_minor` above zero).
- **Savings total** (item 10): `savings_movement` rows + `income.to_savings_minor` + payments on `savings` and `savings_payback` commitments, all cycles, per currency. A `take_out` row is in free money of the cycle in its `cycle_id`; a `prepayment` row (item 14) is not.
- **Prepayment** (item 14): its money out is one `savings_movement` of kind `prepayment` with `prepayment_id`; paid back so far = payments on its `savings_payback` commitments, all cycles.
- **Closing a cycle** (item 12): the money the ended cycle brings in is an `income` row of kind `leftover` in the new cycle (amount = kept + dropped + carried money, or the overspend taken; `to_savings_minor` = saved leftover, so the savings total needs nothing new). `cycle_close` keeps the correction and overspend choice for Past and Undo. Paid it adds a `commitment_payment` to the old commitment and finishes it; Drop marks it `dropped`. These are the only writes to an ended cycle, all in the salary-day batch.
- **Carried over**: the old commitment is marked `carried`; a new row in the new cycle points back with `carried_from_id` (history kept). Salary day (item 11) does the same for last cycle's open commitments due after its expected salary day, with what is still to pay.
- **Instalment plans, card bills, person paybacks, prepayment paybacks**: each makes a `commitment` per cycle, linked by its `*_id`.

## Rules that shape the data (decided in 13)

- **Bills first on conversion**: the rupees of a conversion first cover the spending-currency set-asides not yet covered (the rupees below zero, reserved from the dollars at the cycle's `reserve_rate`); the rate gain or loss is worked out on that part only; the rest is free money.
- **Paid in the other currency** (item 17): a `commitment_payment` in the other currency keeps `covers_minor`, what it covers of the commitment in the commitment's currency at the counting rate when saved (worked out again only when its amount or currency changes). Still to pay = planned − payments in its currency − `covers_minor` of the others; each payment comes out of free money in its own currency.
- **Cards** (item 18): card balance = spends with its `card_id` − what payments on its `card_bill` commitments cover, all cycles; never stored. The phone gets each card's ended-cycles part (`earlierMinor`) and adds the current cycle's rows. A card bill and its payments are left out of free money, set-aside totals, the gap and the leftover (the card spends already came out of free money). A card bill is paid in its own currency only.
- **Ended cycles are locked**: rows with an ended `cycle_id` can't be added, changed or deleted. A forgotten spend is entered in the current cycle; salary-day leftover correction is the only fix.
- **Spends dated back** only within the current cycle.
- **Undo salary day** (item 13): salary day stamps every row it writes or changes with one time, the new cycle's `created_at`, and keeps an undo record in `cycle_close.undo` (JSON): templates it made, changed or deleted (each as it was), pots it made, last cycle's commitments it paid, carried or dropped (with their state before), Paid it's payments, and the step-6 `counting_rate` row if one was typed. Undo is allowed while no row of the user's (spends, incomes, commitments, payments, pots, pot amounts, underspend answers, savings movements, templates, conversions, cards) has `updated_at` after that time, except the new cycle's salary, whose amount stays editable; changing the expected salary day doesn't count either. It deletes the new `cycle` (its incomes, commitments, pot amounts and `cycle_close` cascade), the pots made, the payments, the step-6 counting rate, puts back the templates and reopens the old commitments. Cycles started before item 13 have no record and can't be undone.
- **Delete** is a real delete; the "undo" is only on screen.
- **Offline** (17): the phone only adds `spend` and `commitment_payment` rows and changes its own unsent ones, so no delete markers are needed; a resent row with the same `id` is saved once.
